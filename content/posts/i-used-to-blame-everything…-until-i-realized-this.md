For a long time, I told myself, “*I can’t succeed because my phone is too slow.*”

I blamed my network, my device, even the government.

But one week, something happened… I hit **10K+** views on Facebook in just a few days.

And that’s when I realized my phone was never the problem… I was!

***I was making excuses instead of finding solutions.***

I was waiting for the "perfect time" instead of using the time I already had.

If you’re reading this and you feel stuck, ask yourself:

Is it really your situation holding you back?

Or is it your mindset?

You don’t need perfect tools to start.

You just need to use what you have with the right energy and mindset.

From now on, I choose to stop complaining and start taking action.
