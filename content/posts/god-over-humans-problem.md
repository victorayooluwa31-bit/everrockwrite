I was reading some books, and one of them was based on **problems**. In that book, they explained ***problems as something that is not palatable for human beings, something that brings suffering and pain.*** They further analyzed that all problems have a solution and that everyone has a problem; it just differs from one person to another. However, there's no problem that God doesn't know. He knows everything **(Isaiah 45:7)**. He created them, but He doesn't do evil. But Satan, the only enemy humans have, causes them. Moreover, **sin** causes some problems like unemployment, sickness, death, barrenness, pain, fear, and so on.

Furthermore, *some victories over problems lead to other problems*. For example, someone looking for a job and later getting the job still needs help on how to cope with the work and coworkers. Also, someone's problems might become a solution to another person's life, like a faulty vehicle that constitutes a problem to the owner but brings blessings to the mechanic. It let us know that every problem has a solution and an expiration date, i.e., it is for a certain period. Every problem has a solution, meaning your current problem has a way out. Problems must not leave you the same as they met you; they must change you and your situation. It may either be positive or negative. It can even turn someone wicked or good it just depends on how you handle it and what kind of person you choose to become.

**God will not reveal all the problems we might encounter at once**, but we will face them gradually. Being in a problem is not an indication that God has forgotten or deserted you. With God, every problem is nothing. Seeing someone in a problem doesn't give you the right to mock them because you may later find yourself in their shoes. 

> **Moreover, don't allow any problem to destroy you; rather, let it uplift you. Don't allow a problem to be your master; rather, let it be your servant.** *(Victor Ogunleye)*

In conclusion, dear brethren, many people have problems just like you do. Some people have even bigger problems than yours, and your problems won't last forever. Always remember, **without problems, there is no success**. 

Problems will come, but to overcome them, you must accept Jesus Christ as your personal Savior. You and your family will be saved and free from problems.

You are not the only one facing challenges, God is with you! Shalom!
