**Read:** Judges 16:4–31 (key verses: 16–22)

**Memorise:** ***“But he did not know that the LORD had departed from him.”* (Judges 16:20)**

**Message**

Can a man be strong and still be in danger? Samson was. He was set apart to God from birth as a **Nazirite** **(Judges 13:5)**. The Spirit of the Lord came upon him, and he killed a lion, broke new ropes like thread and carried away the gates of Gaza **(Judges 14:6; 15:14; 16:3)**. Yet Judges 16 shows that this man lost his power. The enemy did not defeat him in battle. He lost it through carelessness.

Samson played with danger. Delilah asked him three times for the secret of his strength, and three times the Philistines were waiting to seize him **(Judges 16:6–14)**. Any wise man would have left after the first betrayal. Samson stayed, and he treated sin as a game. Many believers fall the same way. They do not fall in one day. They get comfortable with what they should have run from **(Proverbs 6:27; 1 Corinthians 10:12)**.

Then he gave away the secret of his calling. “He told her all his heart” (v. 17). He trusted the one person who had shown she could not be trusted. Not everyone close to you is safe with your secrets, and not everyone who smiles with you is for you **(Jeremiah 17:5; Micah 7:5)**.

The saddest verse is this one: ***“I will go out as before, at other times, and shake myself free!”*** He did not know that the Lord had departed from him (v. 20). Samson felt the same, so he thought he was the same. But strength can leave quietly. A person can still pray, preach, sing and serve, and still be running on memory, not on the presence of God (Revelation 3:1). The Philistines then seized him, put out his eyes and bound him in prison (v. 21). Sin takes you further than you planned to go.

Still, the story does not end there. His hair began to grow again (v. 22), and in his last prayer, “O Lord GOD, remember me, I pray!” (v. 28), God answered him. His mercy is real. But do not wait until you are in prison before you take your calling seriously.

**Lessons**

* Do not play with what God has told you to avoid.
* Be careful who you trust with your heart.
* Feeling strong is not proof that God is with you. Stay close to Him.

**Prayer Points**

* Lord, keep me watchful. Do not let me become careless with the grace You have given me.
* Father, deliver me from every Delilah, every habit, place and friendship that is draining my strength.
* Holy Spirit, never depart from me. Renew my strength and keep me close to You.

*The Lord who called you will keep you. Amen. Shalom!*
