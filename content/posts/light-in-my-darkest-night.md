*In the darkness,
Sitting alone,
Thinking about my life
And how it has treated me lately.
I am fed up with everything.*

*I questioned my own faith.
I thought God had left me,
Because I could not hear His voice.
I felt weak.
I was fed up with everything.*

*Problem upon problem,
Trouble upon trouble.
I felt so weak, I did not know what to do.
I cried all night,
I could not sleep.*

*I saw no reason for my existence.
I thought of giving up,
I wanted to end my own life,
Then I remembered what He said:
“I am with you always.”*

*The moment I realised it,
My eyes cleared, my mind was revived.
And I heard a silent voice say,
“My son, do not panic,
For I am with you. Your prayer has been answered*

*Since the day you prayed,
But the time of fulfilment
Has not yet come. If you can be patient,
Your reward will be great.”*

*I then asked,
“Why did You leave me, O Lord?”
He said, “I have never left you, not once.
I am always with you,
But your sins kept you away from Me.”*

*I cried more.
He said, “Cry no more, for your sins
Have been washed away.
You have been forgiven already.
Go and prosper.”*

*I went down on my knees
And praised His holy name.
He has saved me from death.
He has conquered the world;
He is with me always.*

*Ever since then,
I have kept my relationship
With Almighty God,
The One who was in the beginning
And will be in the end.*

*I glorify Your name.*
