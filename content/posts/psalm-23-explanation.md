Have you ever read **Psalm 23** and still didn’t fully understand it? You’re not alone. Many of us read it, quote it, but don’t really know how powerful it is.

This short conversation between Victor and Bola might be the explanation you’ve been waiting for:

**Victor**: (sitting in the living room reading the Bible)

**Bola**: (walks in) Ha Bro Victor, what are you doing with the Bible? (asks surprisingly)

**Victor**: As you can see, I'm reading it.

**Bola**: Where are you reading?

**Victor**: I'm reading Psalm 23.

**Bola**: Wow, I like that Psalm, but I don’t know when or how to use it or even understand it. Can you help me out?

**Victor**: I'm not a teacher nah. Why do you expect me to explain it to you?

**Bola**: Victor please now, I’m serious ooo.

**Victor**: Okay! Psalm 23 talks about how God is our protector, provider, and saviour.

**Bola**: Hmm…

**Victor**: The **first verse** shows God’s love for us. He won't let us lack anything good in life. Just like a shepherd cares for his sheep, God provides for our needs.

**Bola**: Hmm...

**Victor**: The **second verse** shows how God gives us peace and rest. It’s like He’s placing us in a land flowing with comfort and joy, just like Canaan.

**Bola**: Okay...

**Victor**: The **third verse** reminds us that God protects us and guides us. He came down to die for our sins so that we can live eternally.

**Bola**: Hmm.

**Victor**: The **fourth verse** tells us that no matter what we go through—dark times, fear, trouble—God is with us. Even if we walk through the valley of death, we should not be afraid.

**Bola**: Okay, please continue!

**Victor**: The **fifth verse** shows how God blesses us even in the presence of our enemies. He prepares a table for us where others thought we’d fail.

**Bola**: Hmm...

**Victor**: God’s blessings are ours. But we must obey Him, follow His ways, and wait for His time. That’s what the last verse means.

**Bola**: (smiling) Hmm... thank you, Bro Victor.

**Victor**: Have you understood it now?

**Bola**: Yes sir. But where and when can I use it?

**Victor**: Good question! You can use it during prayer, especially morning devotion. You can read it at your workplace before you begin, or when facing challenges. It’s a source of comfort and hope.

**Bola**: Okay sir, thanks for your time. I finally understand it well, after all these years.

**Victor**: You're blessed!

God is always speaking. Sometimes, He just needs someone willing to share His Word simply.

If this blessed you, don’t keep it to yourself—share it with someone who needs peace and hope today.

**Let’s spread the light of Christ, one story at a time.**
