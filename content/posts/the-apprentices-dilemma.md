In a small village nestled in the mountains, there lived a young apprentice named **Kaito**. Kaito was training to become a master craftsman under the guidance of the wise and skilled **Akira.**

Kaito was eager to prove himself and create the most exquisite pieces of furniture the village had ever seen. However, he often found himself frustrated with the slow pace of his progress.

One day, while working on a intricate wooden cabinet, Kaito turned to Akira and said, "Master, I want to create the most beautiful furniture in the village, but I feel like I'm not making progress fast enough."

Akira looked at Kaito with a knowing smile and said, "***To create the tomorrow you want, you have to learn what to do today"***. Focus on mastering the skills required to craft exceptional furniture, and the beauty will follow."

Kaito didn't fully understand Akira's words, but he trusted his master's wisdom. So, he dedicated himself to learning and perfecting his craft.

Days turned into weeks, and weeks turned into months. Kaito's skills improved dramatically, but he still hadn't created the masterpiece he envisioned.

One morning, as he was working on a new project, Kaito had an epiphany. He realized that Akira's words weren't just about mastering skills, but also about patience, persistence, and dedication.

With renewed passion and understanding, Kaito poured his heart and soul into his work. And slowly but surely, his creations began to reflect the beauty and mastery he had always envisioned.

Years later, Kaito became a renowned master craftsman, known throughout the land for his exquisite furniture. And he never forgot the wise words of his master Akira: "To create the tomorrow you want, you have to learn what to do today".
