> *Failure leads to success—if you endure it. But if you give up, failure remains just that: failure.* ~ *Victor Ogunleye.*

Indeed, **failure is the mother of success.**

My dad once told me something profound. He said, "If someone embarks on a project and never faces any challenges or setbacks, that project won’t last. Anything worth achieving must be tested." Think about it—why do people respect successful individuals? It’s not just because they’ve made it; it’s because they once struggled, failed, and persevered.

> *"Success without failure is nothing." ~ Victor Ogunleye.*

Before anyone can call you successful, you must have first tasted failure. It is failure that refines you, strengthens you, and prepares you for lasting success. If there were no exams in school, students wouldn’t understand the true value of their results. In the same way, if there were no failure in life, you would never know your limits. It is failure that reveals your strengths and weaknesses, giving you the chance to improve.

I learned this lesson firsthand. My first order was canceled, and though it was discouraging, it forced me to discover my strengths. I have also failed many times while growing my page, but each failure helped me find the cause of my struggles and overcome them. Success without failure wouldn’t be a story worth telling, and people wouldn’t be able to learn from it. In fact, ***"failure is success itself".*** 

Some people want to try what you did but never even get the chance. If you fail even at the very first step be happy. 

> *Failure is not the end; it’s a lesson.* ~ *Victor Ogunleye.*

Find the cause, learn from it, and don’t let it weigh you down.

Even in the [Bible](https://everrockwrite.netlify.app/posts/the-strongest-careless-man/), this principle is evident. If you read the book of **Revelation** carefully, you’ll see that only after angel Michael and his angels fought tirelessly against Satan was he cast down to Earth. That was a form of failure. But did God stop there? No. According to the book of **John**, God sent His only Son, Jesus Christ, to conquer Satan and redeem humanity. And He succeeded. 

That was when salvation was made available to mankind, and Jesus was given a name above every name. God could have destroyed Satan instantly, but if He had, *would humanity have ever truly understood the power of redemption?* *Would we have even known who God is?*

History is filled with stories of failure turning into greatness. **Elon Musk** failed many times before he finally made his dreams a reality. *Every successful person has once been a failure.* Many of them were school dropouts, some grew up in extremely tough families, and others faced unimaginable hardships. Yet, they never let failure define them. Instead, they used it as fuel to push forward.

> *If you are afraid of failure, you can never be successful.* ~ *Victor Ogunleye.*

Failure is not your enemy; it is your teacher. It gives you the opportunity to restart, to correct your mistakes, and to grow. But giving up? That takes everything away from you.

> *"Failure is important in the journey of success." ~ Victor Ogunleye.*

When you fail, don’t despair. Analyze the cause, find the solution, and try again. 

Always remember: **Failure is the mother of success.**
