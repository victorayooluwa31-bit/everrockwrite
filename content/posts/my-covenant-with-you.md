God is my shepherd,
I shall not want.
In the day of my darkness,
He showed up for me.

When life is tough,
With no one to help me,
I call upon His only name,
And He responds with thunder.

He says,
Son, fear not,
For I am with you,
And everything you need
Has already been provided.

Just keep trusting Me,
For I have done it.
Very soon, it will be announced.
You have My word,
Which cannot return to Me
Without fulfilling its purpose.

And this will be My covenant with you:
You will not be put to shame,
And you will not want.
I show mercy to My very own people,
And you are among them.

And because you trust Me,
I won’t let you down.
Go and prosper.
