**Read:** Genesis 37; 39–41; 45:1–8 (key verses: Genesis 39:2; 50:20)

**Memorise:** *“The LORD was with Joseph, and he was a successful man.”* (Genesis 39:2)

**Message**

Can a man be betrayed, forgotten and imprisoned, and still be called blessed? Joseph was. His own brothers hated him and sold him for twenty shekels of silver **(Genesis 37:28)**. He was a slave in a strange land, accused falsely and thrown into prison **(Genesis 39:20)**. Yet the Bible says the Lord was with him in every place. Joseph’s life is a story of faith, endurance and divine elevation.

Look at what he faced. He was betrayed by his own family. He was tempted by Potiphar’s wife day after day, and he ran away, saying, “***How then can I do this great wickedness, and sin against God?***” **(Genesis 39:9)**. He suffered injustice when he did right and was rewarded with prison. Many people today would have given up, hardened their hearts or made compromises. Joseph kept his trust in God.

Notice how he handled it. He was patient while he waited in prison and God’s timing seemed slow **(Psalm 105:17–19)**. He kept his integrity when no one was watching **(Genesis 39:8–12)**. And when his brothers stood before him, he chose forgiveness: “***You meant evil against me; but God meant it for good***” **(Genesis 50:20)**. In one day God raised him from prison to the palace **(Genesis 41:14, 41),** because his character had been prepared before his position came.

Now ask yourself **where is the Joseph of this generation?** Where are the young people who will hold on to purity when it costs them something? Who will serve faithfully in the dark place, forgive those who wronged them, and trust God to lift them in due time (1 Peter 5:6)? Can we find a Joseph in this generation?

In the coming days, I will be sharing a series of reflections on Joseph’s journey, looking at his challenges, his character and the lessons we can apply today.

**Lessons**

* Betrayal, temptation and injustice do not cancel God’s plan.
* Integrity matters most when no one is watching.
* Forgiveness opens the door to what God has prepared.

**Prayer Points**

* Lord, give me the faith to trust You when life feels unfair.
* Father, help me keep my integrity under every temptation.
* Holy Spirit, teach me to forgive and to wait for Your perfect time.

***The Lord who was with Joseph will be with you. Amen.***
