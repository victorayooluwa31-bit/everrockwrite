Back when I was working on my final-year project at school, there was a guy in my department, **Philip**, who was under the same supervisor as me. There was also a girl named **Mary**, and she is simply beautiful.

At the start of the project, the two of them barely spoke. But as we got closer to the end, they suddenly began talking, and they haven’t stopped since. They’re not dating. It’s all just for fun, but we started calling them husband and wife anyway.

Since then, this boy no get sense at all 😂. He gives Mary so much attention ❤️‍🔥 that if you were new to the department, you’d swear something was going on between them. Nothing is. He sits beside her, and they gist like a real couple 💏. Honestly, they act more married than some actual couples.

If we try to tease Mary, Philip stands up to defend her. If anyone says anything about her, he’s ready to fight on her behalf. If any guy is disturbing her, he steps in immediately. None of it is a real fight, just playful drama, but if you didn’t know them, you’d think it was serious.

Sometimes we joke, ***“If una two dey date for real, oppression go just kill us for here!*** 😂***”***

The care he shows her is honestly more than what you see in many real relationships. In my opinion, if they ever did date, it would be a perfect match. Who knows what the future holds for them? Whatever happens, they’re both great people.
