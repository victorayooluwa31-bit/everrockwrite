A year later, Daniel had become an important part of the farm's operations.

His idea had grown into a small independent enterprise operating in partnership with Mr. Adeyemi.

He began supplying processed feed ingredients to farmers across neighbouring communities.

His income improved, his debts were gradually cleared, and he eventually employed some of the workers he had been forced to dismiss when his first business collapsed.

One afternoon, while attending an agricultural business exhibition, Daniel saw a familiar face.

It was Michael.

The same friend who had disappeared with his money.

Michael looked uncomfortable when their eyes met.

"Daniel!" he exclaimed, extending his hand. "My brother! How have you been?"

Daniel looked at him for a moment.

"I'm doing well."

Michael glanced at the business banner behind him.

"I heard about your new company. Congratulations, man. We should sit down sometime. There are opportunities we can explore together."

Daniel smiled faintly.

"Opportunities?"

"Yes, my brother. We made mistakes in the past, but that's behind us now."

Daniel shook his head.

"Michael, the past may be behind us, but the lessons are still with us."

Michael's smile disappeared.

"I know I made a mistake."

"You didn't just make a mistake. You betrayed someone who trusted you. But I've moved on."

Daniel turned to leave, then paused.

"One thing I learned from losing that money is that not everyone who walks beside you is walking in the same direction. I wish you well, but I will not repeat the same lesson twice."

He walked away.
