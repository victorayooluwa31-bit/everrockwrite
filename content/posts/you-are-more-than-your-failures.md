I used to think failure was the end. Every time I failed, I felt like I wasn’t good enough.

I remember a time when I worked so hard on a project, hoping it would bring success. But instead, I lost everything. No money, no results, just regret. I wanted to disappear, to hide from the embarrassment.

But one day, I read something that changed my life: “***Failure is not the opposite of success. It is part of success.***”

That’s when I stopped running from my failures and started learning from them.

I realized every failure was teaching me something patience, resilience, and the power of standing back up.

Now, when I fail, I don’t see it as an ending. I see it as a lesson. And every lesson brings me closer to the success I once thought was impossible.

So, if you’re feeling like a failure, remember this: **You are not your mistakes**. You are your comeback.

Get up. Keep going. Your success story is still being written.
