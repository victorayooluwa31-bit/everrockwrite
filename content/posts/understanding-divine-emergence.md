**Text: Ecclesiastes 3:1**

**Key Scriptures: Genesis 37–50, Romans 8:28, James 1:2–4.**

# INTRODUCTION

When we talk about **Divine Emergence**, we are talking about a deep spiritual principle. Before God brings anyone into the open, He first prepares that person. During this period of preparation, God shapes the person's character, strengthens their faith, and equips them for the assignment ahead.

We can see this in the life of **Joseph**. Before he became the Prime Minister of Egypt, he went through many difficult experiences. He was hated by his brothers, sold into slavery, falsely accused, imprisoned, and forgotten. Yet, through every stage, God was preparing him. Joseph remained faithful and did not seek revenge against those who hurt him. Instead, he trusted God's plan and later became a blessing to many people.

In this lecture, we will learn what Divine Emergence means, why God brings His people into emergence, and how believers should respond when God brings them into their season of emergence.

**May the Holy Spirit give us understanding as we study His Word, and may our lives never remain the same, in Jesus' name. Amen.**

## **WHAT IS EMERGENCE?**

According to the **Oxford Dictionary**, emergence is the process of coming into existence, becoming visible, or developing for the first time.

Divine Emergence is the process by which God brings out His prepared servants from hiddenness into the place of His purpose at His appointed time. **(Galatians 4:4, 1 John 3:8).**

God never brings anyone into emergence by accident. Before He reveals a person, He first prepares that person.

## **WHY DOES GOD BRING PEOPLE INTO EMERGENCE?**

**God brings people into emergence to:**

* Destroy the works of the devil. (1 John 3:8)
* Deliver His people from bondage and oppression. (Exodus 3:7–10)
* Preserve and rescue lives in times of need. (Genesis 50:19–20)
* Preach the Gospel to every nation. (Mathews 28:19–20; Mark 16:15)
* Lead people to salvation through Jesus Christ. (John 3:16–17; Acts 4:12)
* Teach people God's ways and lead them in righteousness. (Mathews 5:14–16; Psalm 32:8)
* Bring the lost back to God. (Luke 19:10; Luke 15:4–7)
* Fulfil His divine purpose and bring Him glory. (Ephesians 2:10; Isaiah 43:7)

## HOW SHOULD BELIEVERS RESPOND WHEN GOD BRINGS THEM INTO EMERGENCE?

**Believers should:**

* Obey God's voice and follow His leading. (John 10:27; Deuteronomy 28:1)
* Trust God instead of depending on their own understanding. (Proverbs 3:5–6)
* Remain humble and give all the glory to God. (James 4:10; 1 Peter 5:6)
* Stay faithful and live a holy life. (1 Peter 1:15–16; Hebrews 12:14)
* Use their gifts and position to serve others, not themselves. (Mathews 20:26–28; 1 Peter 4:10)
* Continue to grow spiritually through prayer, God's Word, and fellowship. (Joshua 1:8; Acts 2:42; 2 Timothy 2:15)
* Never forget the purpose for which God brought them into emergence. (Esther 4:14; Philippians 3:13–14)

## **CONCLUSION**

Divine Emergence is not about becoming popular or successful; it is about fulfilling God's purpose at His appointed time. Before God reveals a person, He prepares that person.

Therefore, when God brings us into our season of emergence, we must remain faithful, humble, and committed to His purpose so that our lives will bring glory to Him.
