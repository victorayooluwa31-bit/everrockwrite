My name is **VICTOR OGUNLEYE**. I come from a humble family of seven. I am the first son and the eldest male child, although I have an older sister. Growing up, I often wondered how people could live in a world free from problems, but I never found an answer. What I did discover, however, was my passion and talent for writing.

I discovered my talent for writing when I joined an organization at the age of 20. Before then, I had always been passionate about preaching and sharing encouraging words with people, a passion I developed when I was about 17 or even earlier. I had even written a storybook, but because of my lack of self-confidence, I could not bring myself to show it to anyone.

My journey into writing was not an easy one. One of my biggest challenges was the absence of someone to guide or mentor me. I had to learn on my own by reading other people's articles and posts online and picking up whatever knowledge I could. I was also a shy person who struggled with confidence. I often felt that people would not appreciate my writing, so I underestimated myself and kept many of my thoughts and lessons to myself. Despite that, I constantly found myself discovering new topics to write about, particularly those centered on motivation, inspiration, and life lessons.

When I turned 20, things gradually began to change. Through the organization I joined, I started developing more confidence in myself and my ability to write. I created a [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/share/1C3nxntxkW/) page where I began sharing my thoughts and writings every day. However, I struggled to remain consistent because I was discouraged by the lack of likes, comments, and engagement on my posts.

Then, in 2025, I shared a personal story on my Facebook page. It was about my own mistakes and experiences, which I hoped others could learn from. Unfortunately, one of my family members came across the post, and the situation escalated, creating tension within my family. Although I had not intended to attack or expose anyone, my family misunderstood my intentions, and it led to several problems. I was even told not to post on my page again.

That experience hurt me deeply. I felt alone, with no one to support my dream or understand what I was trying to achieve. I had always dreamed of becoming a motivational speaker and mentor, using my words to encourage people, guide them through life's challenges, and spread the Gospel across the world. Yet, at that point, I felt as though nobody believed in that dream.

Eventually, I lost much of the confidence I had worked so hard to build. To make matters worse, my Facebook page, which had grown to 5,000 followers, was banned, and I lost everything I had built there. It was painful, especially because I had invested so much time and effort into it.

After that experience, I created an account on [Medium](https://medium.com/@victorayooluwa.31), hoping to continue sharing my thoughts and writings. However, I struggled with consistency because of the disappointments and setbacks I had experienced. Nevertheless, whenever a new topic came to mind, I would work on it, complete my writing, and save it somewhere safe for future use. Even when I was not publishing, I never completely stopped writing.

Everything began to change when I met a beautiful young lady. The first time she read my writing, she was amazed by it and encouraged me not to give up. She became the first person to genuinely support and believe in my writing journey. Her encouragement meant a lot to me, and it inspired me to start writing poems for her.

Today, I have decided to take another step by expanding my writing beyond social media and building my own blog website, **[EverRock](https://everrockstudio.netlify.app/) Write**. I want to create a space where I can freely share my personal life stories, biblical reflections, poems, fictional stories, and other meaningful writings.

**My mission** is simple: to spread words of encouragement across the world and help people navigate through life. I want to use my talent to inspire, motivate, teach, and share the Gospel, reaching people through the power of words.

This is not my first attempt at building a platform. In 2025, I also created a website using **[WordPress](https://wordpress.com/)**, but unfortunately, that website was banned too. Despite these setbacks, I have chosen not to give up.

I believe there is still so much I can do with the gift of writing, and I am determined to keep growing, learning, and sharing my words with the world.

> **I will not give up until I achieve my goal and the world sees the light in my words.**
