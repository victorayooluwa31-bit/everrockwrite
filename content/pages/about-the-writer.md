Hello, I’m Victor Ogunleye. I’m a student, a writer, and a man who fears God. I believe in respecting both God and people, and I try to live by that every day.

My life has not been easy. I have walked through difficult seasons, times when problems piled up, when I felt weak, and when I wondered whether God had forgotten me. But in those moments I learned that He never left. Every hard night taught me something about faith, patience and hope, and those lessons are what I bring to my writing.

Writing is my passion, and it has also been my way of healing. On EverRock Write, I share what I learn from the Word of God, the poems that come to me, and stories about life, love and faith. Some of them come from my own struggles, and some come from the people around me.

I don’t write because I have it all figured out. I write because I know what it feels like to be tired, to be confused, and to need a word that lifts you up. If something I write encourages you, challenges you or makes you think, then it has done its job.

Thank you for being here. Whatever you are going through, you are not alone.
