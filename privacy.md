This page explains what information this website collects and how it is used. It is written in plain language and applies to everyone who visits.

## What this site collects

**Page view counts.** The site uses GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to count how many times each page is viewed. It does not use cookies, it does not build a profile of you, and it does not follow you across other websites. It records general details such as the page viewed, the country, the browser and the device type.

**Nothing else by default.** There are no accounts, no comment forms and no sign-up forms on this site, so it does not collect your name or email address.

## Fonts and outside links

The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser makes a request to Google when a page loads. Share buttons (WhatsApp, X, Facebook) only open those services when you click them. Once you leave this site, the privacy policy of that service applies.

## Cookies

This site does not set cookies of its own.

## If you contact me

If you send a message or email, the details you share are used only to reply to you and are not passed on to anyone else.

## Your choices

You can block analytics in your browser at any time with a content blocker, and the site will still work normally.

## Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version is always on this page.

## Questions

For any question about this policy, use the contact details on the About page.
