title: Copyright and Ownership
---
All original writing on this website, including posts, poems, lyrics, stories, images and the site design, belongs to its author and is protected by copyright. All rights reserved.

## What you may do

- Share a link to any post, on social media, in messages or by email.
- Quote a short passage, as long as you credit the author and link back to the original post.
- Read, print or save a post for your own personal use.

## What needs permission

- Republishing a full post, poem, song or story anywhere else, in print or online.
- Recording, performing or setting any poem or lyric to music, or using it in a video, book, product or advertisement.
- Translating or adapting a piece and publishing the result.
- Using any image or the site logo.

To ask for permission, use the contact details on the About page. Please say which piece you want to use and how.

## Scripture and other quotations

Bible quotations are credited to their translation and belong to their respective publishers. Quotations from other writers remain the property of those writers and are used with credit.

## Reporting a problem

If you believe something on this site uses your work without permission, get in touch through the About page and it will be looked into promptly.
