This is a sample post. Open `/admin` on your site, log in with GitHub, and you can edit or delete it.

## What you can do in a post

Add headings, **bold** and *italic* text, lists, quotes and images.

> Pressing Enter once inside a paragraph keeps the line break, which is handy for poems and lyrics.

To upload an image, use the image button in the editor, or set a cover image in the Image field.
